Effect pack art (img/fxp/)
-------------------------
Nine pack-effect animations, 8 frames each (72 PNG files). Used only when a button
turns on "Pack effect" and picks a Kind. The Forge fire/frost/arcane/bolt art is
unchanged.

  Kenney Particle Pack (CC0 / Public Domain)
    https://kenney.nl/assets/particle-pack
    Author: Kenney Vleugels (Kenney.nl)
    Used for: Blaze, Ice glints, Crackle, Rune circle, Holy light, Poison, Smoke
    Changes: Kenney's black-background sprites were converted to white luminance
    glyphs and composited into 8-frame, 96x96 rim / disc loops for hotbuttons.

  2D Spell Effects (CC0 / Public Domain)
    https://opengameart.org/content/2d-spell-effects
    Author: the OpenGameArt uploader of this page (made for the aRPG Nyrthos, www.nyrthos.com)
    Used for: Storm orb (from the lighteningBall strip)
    Changes: frames were subsampled to 8 and resized into a 96x96 canvas.

  Multiple Colors of Sparkle Animations (CC0 / Public Domain)
    https://opengameart.org/content/multiple-colors-of-sparkle-animations
    Author: VWolfdog (opengameart.org)
    Used for: Sparkles (White strip)
    Changes: frames were subsampled to 8 and resized into a 96x96 canvas.

Other packs considered but not shipped: Magic and Smoke Effect (StumpyStrust, CC0)
was too large (1024-4096 sheet tiles) for a hotbutton frame budget; Basic Pixel
Animated Spells and StarsteelGaming Spell Effects are CC0 but too small / too
directional for a rim overlay.
